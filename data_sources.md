## 3. Dataset Selection Notes

- Indian mandi prices are preferred for the AtmoSync Indian market model.
- International avocado datasets may be used only as reference data.
- Dataset geography, date range, columns, and licensing must be verified before implementation.
- No unverified data will be treated as actual Indian market prices.

## 4. Important Limitation

The identified Kaggle avocado dataset may represent US retail prices rather than Indian wholesale mandi prices. It will be used only for reference analysis until its geographical coverage and data structure are verified.
